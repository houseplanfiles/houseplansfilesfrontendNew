# HousePlanFiles

HousePlanFiles is India's leading platform for readymade house plans, architectural designs, and construction services.
We connect users with architects, contractors, building material suppliers, and interior design shops in their cities.

## Core Offerings
- **Readymade House Plans**: 1000s of ready-to-use floor plans, 3D elevations, and complete blueprints.
- **Architects & Interior Designers**: Discover and hire top-rated professionals.
- **Contractors**: Find reliable building contractors, electrical contractors, plumbers, etc.
- **Marketplace**: Buy building materials, home decor, and construction equipment from verified local shops and manufacturers.
- **Lead Board**: A marketplace for professionals to purchase leads and connect with potential clients.

## Technology Stack
- Frontend: Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, Redux Toolkit
- Backend: Node.js, Express, MongoDB
- Payments: Razorpay
